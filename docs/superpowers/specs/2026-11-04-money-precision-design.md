## Approach
A per-input numericRule: moneyCents opts into an exact decimal-to-safe-integer-cent comparison. The three dedicated factories explicitly opt in through the existing private question builder; the unused legacy mixed factory keeps its previous grading. Other generators remain untagged and keep their current rules, including unit conversion, discrete facts and AP2 custom graders.

Relative percentage tolerance is unsuitable for this approved monetary task. Rounding the learner's answer before comparison would silently forgive an incorrect fractional cent. Chosen implementation therefore compares exact decimal cent amounts, independently of binary floating-point conversion. Preserve the current full-number grammar: decimal comma OR point, signs, scientific notation, surrounding whitespace, trailing zeroes. No thousands grouping, euro signs, malformed or partial numbers, nonfinite values. Numerically identical representations are equivalent; adjacent cents and nonzero fractional cents are rejected. Safe integer cents bound the helper; current generated amounts are far below that bound. Huge exponents must not allocate huge strings; nonzero underflow must not become zero.

The generator already rounds every new monetary line to cents and carries that amount forward. Keep the existing net/gross bases and independent integer-cent row oracle unchanged. Negative differential gaps compare by exact sign/cents. Positive half-cent generator ties remain rounded per the established calculation chain; fractional-cent learner inputs are not silently rounded. The hint selector uses each field's actual config and offers a neutral cent-precision hint for fractional-cent input, never a guessed mental cause. No hint for accepted answers, including accepted legacy tolerance fixtures.
